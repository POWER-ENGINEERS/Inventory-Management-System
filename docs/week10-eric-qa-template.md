# Week 10 — Eric Contribution: QA Evidence Template

This document adds a structured template for recording manual QA results without changing application behavior.

| Scenario | Reproduction steps | Expected result | Actual result | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example | Describe exact steps | Describe expected behavior | Record observed behavior | P0/P1/P2 | PASS/FAIL |

## QA recording rules

- Record exact reproduction steps.
- Keep expected and actual results separate.
- Assign P0/P1/P2 only after confirming the observed impact.
- Do not mark a scenario PASS without performing the test.
- During the Week 10 feature freeze, record discovered application bugs rather than fixing them in the QA pass.
